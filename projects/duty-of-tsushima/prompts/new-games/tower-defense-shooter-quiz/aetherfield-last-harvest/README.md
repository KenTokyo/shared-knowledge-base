# Aetherfield: Last Harvest

## Direction

- Premium sunlit fantasy-tech farm defense with a warm handcrafted heart.
- A small voxel-like hero moves through a tactile 3D diorama rather than commanding from a board.
- Everyday farm materials become credible defenses through brass aether mechanisms.
- The emotional curve moves from calm morning preparation to a radiant golden-hour siege.
- This is the recommended all-round direction because farm, quiz, hero, lane, and effects read clearly.

## Visual Pillars

1. **Handmade before futuristic:** timber joinery, rope, stone, ceramic, canvas, and brass carry form.
2. **Aether is an accent:** turquoise energy appears only at active cores, trails, shields, and answers.
3. **Sunlight owns the image:** warm key, cool fill, crisp contact, dusty volumetric depth.
4. **Small hero, large consequence:** compact articulated figures animate with weight and precise recoil.
5. **Farm remains a farm:** crops, irrigation, fences, sheds, paths, tools, and weathering survive upgrades.

## Camera and Composition

- Elevated three-quarter camera at 52–58° pitch.
- Hero occupies roughly 7–10% of frame height in ordinary combat.
- Core remains a strong middle-ground landmark without sitting under HUD.
- Lanes form broad S-curves through crops and fence gates.
- Foreground uses low crops/fence edges; middle ground holds action; hills and windmills close depth.
- Avoid pure orthographic view, miniature tilt-shift blur, or isometric grid lock.

## World Construction

- Map 1: `Amberstep Farm`, a terraced grain and orchard holding around a blue-stone archive core.
- Map 2 preview: `Tideglass Paddies`, flooded terraces, bridges, water lanes, and mist turbines.
- Map 3 preview: `Highwind Orchard`, cliff farm, rope lifts, windbreak walls, and glider enemies.
- Lifetime map cap remains three.
- Buildings are asymmetrical working sheds, mills, greenhouses, and field shrines.
- No direct historical location, copied shrine, branded machinery, or theme-park Japanese clutter.

## Defense Language

- Fence upgrades progress from timber/rope to stone-brass braces to aether-latched gates.
- Sentry resembles a compact brass farm tool with a visible feed and recoil arm.
- Mortar resembles a ceramic seed hopper on a stout timber frame.
- Arc Coil uses three prongs and a turquoise charge gap.
- Frost Fan uses blue ceramic vanes and condensation.
- Repair Beacon uses a slow windwheel and articulated repair motes.
- Traps remain flush to soil and telegraph direction through physical geometry.

## Character Language

- Field Ranger: indigo field jacket, pale segmented armour, compact carbine, brass tool pack.
- Elemental Technician: ceramic gauntlets, aether canisters, short coat, small hovering survey drone.
- Bodies are compact but not cubic mascots; head, torso, pelvis, limbs, hands, and feet remain separated.
- Animation uses planted feet, hip/chest counter-rotation, shoulder lead, recoil recovery, and tool weight.
- Faces are simple stylized planes with eyes and brows; no realistic skin close-ups.

## UI Language

- Warm translucent parchment panels over thin dark backing for readability.
- Beaten-brass rails define groups; indigo tabs carry selected state.
- Cyan appears for valid/charged/correct; vermilion for invalid/danger/wrong.
- Condensed humanist sans for data; restrained serif only for title and quiz question.
- Decorative grain stays outside text fields.
- The world remains visible around compact corner HUD clusters.

## Effect Language

- Ballistics kick dust, splinters, brass sparks, leaves, and short smoke puffs.
- Aether effects have a dense pale core, turquoise edge, controlled bloom, and physical contact.
- Fire chars crops and soil cosmetically; frost gathers on surfaces; lightning grounds through metal.
- Shockwaves move dust and grass with bounded field response.
- No screen-filling cyan fog, flat magic circles, or bloom-only damage.

## Known Risk

- Warm farm detail can become visually busy; protect lane values and cap decorative props near combat.
- Cyan energy can overwhelm natural materials; reserve it for active information.
- Parchment UI can become fantasy-generic; brass mechanism lines and modern data spacing keep it original.

## Choose This Direction When

- First-session readability matters more than a dark dramatic mood.
- The farm should feel worth protecting before combat starts.
- The product should welcome action, strategy, and quiz audiences equally.
- The implementation team wants material variety without a reflection-heavy night pipeline.
