# Blackrain Protocol — Asset Manifest

## Shared Generation Rules

- Built-in image generation, one independent call per image.
- Use case: `stylized-concept`, except explicit interface screens use `ui-mockup`.
- Intended output: polished 16:9 landscape game-development reference.
- Keep the same storm-night art direction, operator silhouettes, Vault, materials, and camera family.
- Elevated tactical 3D perspective with compact bevelled voxel-like characters and credible weapon stance.
- Original designs only; no brand marks, copied military UI, famous characters, watermarks, or signatures.
- Catalogue images show at most six isolated designs.
- Gameplay scenes use cyan friendly, amber selection, and red hostile signals consistently.
