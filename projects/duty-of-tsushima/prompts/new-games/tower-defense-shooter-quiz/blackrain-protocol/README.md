# Blackrain Protocol

## Direction

- Tactical storm-night defense on a hardened agricultural research site.
- A compact operator moves through wet crop rows, steel barriers, floodlights, and sensor towers.
- Gunplay and readable threat telemetry lead; quiz knowledge is framed as a live command protocol.
- The mood is intense and premium without copying any military game interface or iconography.
- This is the strongest direction when shooter impact and dark atmosphere define the brand.

## Visual Pillars

1. **Wet physical world:** rain, puddles, mud, tarps, water trails, and grounded reflections.
2. **Industrial farm:** irrigation rigs, crop tunnels, silos, grow lamps, barriers, and service roads.
3. **Signal discipline:** cyan system, amber selection, red danger; no uncontrolled rainbow neon.
4. **Compact operator weight:** small stylized voxel proportions with realistic weapon stance and recoil.
5. **Threat hierarchy:** enemies, lanes, core, build ghost, and boss attacks survive darkness and smoke.

## Camera and Composition

- Elevated tactical chase camera at 55–62° pitch with controlled perspective.
- Slightly tighter follow distance than Aetherfield.
- Hero occupies roughly 9–12% of frame height.
- Rain and reflections guide lines toward the Quiz Vault, never obscure it.
- Floodlit lane islands create alternating safe/readable and dangerous/dark zones.
- Avoid security-camera fisheye, pure top-down view, or excessive camera shake.

## World Construction

- Map 1: `Blackrain Grow-Site`, fortified crop research farm around an obsidian data vault.
- Map 2 preview: `Floodplain Array`, half-submerged polytunnels, pump bridges, and current hazards.
- Map 3 preview: `Coldframe Ridge`, snowbound greenhouse terraces, wind shutters, and ice lanes.
- Lifetime map cap remains three.
- Structures use steel, concrete, composite panel, fabric, glass, irrigation pipe, and real foundations.
- No copied military base, logo, insignia, real weapon brand, or proprietary faction language.

## Defense Language

- Fence upgrades progress from welded mesh to composite blast rail to powered anti-breach barrier.
- Sentry uses a compact stabilized receiver and visible magazine feed.
- Mortar is a squat smart-fertilizer launcher with armoured cradle.
- Arc Coil is an insulated three-post transformer with grounding cables.
- Frost Fan is a cryogenic crop-preservation turbine.
- Repair Beacon is a maintenance mast with two mechanical drones.
- Traps use physical plates, cables, cones, mines, and pressure pistons.

## Character Language

- Field Ranger: dark weather shell, composite plates, amber magazine markers, compact rifle.
- Elemental Technician: ceramic insulation, coil gauntlets, cyan reactor, cable harness, utility drone.
- Voxel-like forms use bevelled modules and readable limbs, not cubic toy people.
- Weapon handling carries shoulder contact, muzzle climb, magazine weight, and fast clean recovery.
- Faces remain stylized under rain hoods or half masks; no real military identity or likeness.

## UI Language

- Smoked glass plates with opaque charcoal backing under text.
- Thin metal rails, strong grid, precise alignment, waveform and lane telemetry.
- Cyan means online/valid, amber means selected/caution, red means imminent failure.
- Condensed sans only; large numeric hierarchy; no fake microtext.
- HUD clusters live at corners and preserve the center combat cone.
- Quiz question expands across the top but physical answer pylons remain the primary interaction.

## Effect Language

- Ballistics produce short muzzle gas, wet soil kick, sparks, impact smoke, and casing motion.
- Lasers expose heat, ionized air, contact bloom, steam, and surface scorch.
- Lightning visibly follows grounded metal and water with capped branches.
- Explosions push rain and smoke outward; puddles ripple at contact.
- Red is reserved for hostile telegraphs, never decorative ambience.
- No persistent full-screen haze, neon outline on every object, or giant transparent rectangles.

## Known Risk

- Darkness can erase crops and lanes; every gameplay material needs a stable midtone and wet rim.
- Tactical UI can become decorative noise; every line or graph needs a real state owner.
- Reflections, rain, smoke, and lights compete for frame time; quality tiers must preserve target clarity.

## Choose This Direction When

- Strong weapon feel and combat intensity are the leading promise.
- A serious high-tech mood is preferred over cozy fantasy.
- The team can budget wet surfaces, weather, smoke, and controlled local lights.
- Quiz interaction should feel like operating a pressured defense protocol.
