# Wildcore: Bloom Siege

## Direction

- A bright living solarpunk farm grows defenses around a radiant seed-memory archive.
- Voxel-like heroes are expressive field guardians, not soldiers dropped into a fantasy palette.
- Towers combine plant growth, ceramic engineering, water pressure, wind, crystals, and luminous sap.
- The world visibly reacts to elemental skills while gameplay terrain remains stable.
- This is the most original direction and gives the Elemental Technician the strongest visual home.

## Visual Pillars

1. **Life is structure:** roots, vines, woven reed, ceramic, stone, and water carry mechanical roles.
2. **Color follows function:** coral core, jade nature, blue water/frost, gold electricity, violet gravity.
3. **Motion everywhere, noise nowhere:** leaves, petals, irrigation, fabric, and creatures move in layers.
4. **Readable small heroes:** rounded-faceted voxel forms use broad gestures and clear class silhouettes.
5. **Earned bloom:** the empty farm becomes a dense defensive garden through player choices.

## Camera and Composition

- Elevated three-quarter camera at 50–56° pitch with a slightly wider tactical view.
- Hero occupies roughly 7–9% of frame height.
- Curved roots and irrigation paths lead toward the Wildcore Seed Archive.
- Elemental zones use clear silhouettes and transparent interiors so enemies remain visible.
- Foreground leaves frame without crossing aim or HUD zones.
- Avoid flat cartoon orthographic staging, candy saturation, or opaque particle carpets.

## World Construction

- Map 1: `Bloomstead Basin`, circular mixed-crop farm around a living seed-memory tree.
- Map 2 preview: `Cloudvine Steps`, floating terrace edges, wind bridges, and vertical draft hazards.
- Map 3 preview: `Emberroot Caldera`, warm volcanic soil, mineral irrigation, ash bloom, and magma vents.
- Lifetime map cap remains three.
- Structures grow from authored root frames reinforced by glazed ceramic and woven fibre.
- No copied fantasy village, famous tree, branded creature, or generic mushroom-world clutter.

## Defense Language

- Fence upgrades progress from woven thorn to root lattice to sap-lit living bastion.
- Sentry is a seed-spitter blossom with articulated ceramic barrel petals.
- Mortar is a bulb catapult that visibly swells before launching a heavy seed pod.
- Arc Coil is a crystal-vine trellis with capped lightning branches.
- Frost Fan is a broad dew-flower that emits visible cold mist directionally.
- Repair Beacon is a pollinator lantern that sends small restorative motes to structures.
- Traps read as roots, pollen pods, pressure reeds, decoy blooms, and mineral plates.

## Character Language

- Field Ranger: leaf-cloth jacket, ceramic plates, seed-metal carbine, utility satchel.
- Elemental Technician: petal vane shoulders, crystal gauntlets, sap reactor, hovering pollinator drone.
- Proportions are compact and expressive with hands large enough to sell aiming and device recoil.
- Locomotion uses planted feet, squash only in soft gear, and broad readable shoulder/hip motion.
- Faces are stylized masks with warm eyes and brows; no chibi baby proportions.

## UI Language

- Seed-vault panels combine solid dark bark backing, pale ceramic faces, and petal-shaped tabs.
- Coral marks core state, jade marks growth, gold marks earned choice, violet marks high threat.
- Friendly rounded sans for labels; sturdy condensed numerals for resources and waves.
- Icons use silhouette plus a small pattern, never color alone.
- UI stays compact; animated vines never grow across text or the combat center.
- Quiz question appears as a clean seed archive ribbon while answers remain physical petals/pylons.

## Effect Language

- Fire curls through dry leaves then leaves bounded char and ember decay.
- Frost crystallizes dew and makes brittle facets before melting into droplets.
- Lightning travels crystal-vine paths and grounds through water with capped branching.
- Wind reveals force through petals, grass, dust, and pressure ribbons.
- Gravity bends pollen or leaf motion around a dense core without hiding targets.
- No generic recolored circles, bloom-only spells, or endless particle accumulation.

## Known Risk

- Organic silhouettes can blur tower roles; every defense needs a unique hard profile and muzzle.
- Saturated effects can flatten depth; preserve neutral materials and localize emissive color.
- Vegetation motion can become visual noise; layer amplitude by distance and combat relevance.

## Choose This Direction When

- A distinctive elemental fantasy identity matters more than tactical realism.
- The farm should visibly heal, grow, and remember the player's choices.
- The team wants living towers and material-reactive spells as the leading visual hook.
- A bright all-ages surface with deep build strategy fits the target audience.
